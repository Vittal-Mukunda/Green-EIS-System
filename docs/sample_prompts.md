# Instructions

You will receive environmental disclosure data for several companies, taken from their annual Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reports (BRSR). Company names are masked: each company is identified only by the code in the heading of its block.

- Use only the evidence given in this message. Do not use any outside knowledge about companies, sectors or the world.
- Assess each company independently of the others.
- All figures are for fiscal year FY2024-25 unless a column says otherwise. Figures are rounded as printed; use them exactly as printed and do not recompute them.
- Give no text outside the JSON array described under "Output".

## Derived KPIs

Each derived KPI is computed from the reported indicators as follows.

- Energy intensity: Total energy consumption / Revenue from operations (unit: GJ per INR crore)
- Emissions intensity (Scope 1+2): (Scope 1 emissions + Scope 2 emissions) / Revenue from operations (unit: tCO2e per INR crore)
- Water intensity: Water consumption / Revenue from operations (unit: kL per INR crore)
- Renewable energy share: 100 * Renewable energy consumption / Total energy consumption (unit: %)
- Waste recovery rate: 100 * Waste recovered / Waste generated (unit: %)

## Task 1: t2_class (apply the rule)

Give each company a class, Leader, Average or Laggard, by applying the rule below exactly. The rule uses only the FY2024-25 values of the three KPIs named in it, as printed in the company's block, and the cut-points of the company's own sector.

For each of the 3 KPIs in the table, compare the company's FY2024-25 value with the low and high cut-point of the company's own sector (listed below) and give the KPI a score. The value is the one printed in the company's block; do not recompute it.

| KPI | Better when | Score 2 | Score 1 | Score 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emissions intensity (Scope 1+2) | lower | at most the low cut-point | above the low cut-point and at most the high cut-point | above the high cut-point |
| Water intensity | lower | at most the low cut-point | above the low cut-point and at most the high cut-point | above the high cut-point |
| Renewable energy share | higher | at least the high cut-point | at least the low cut-point and below the high cut-point | below the low cut-point |

Add the scores to get a total from 0 to 6. Total 5 to 6: Leader. Total 2 to 4: Average. Total 0 to 1: Laggard. A value exactly equal to a cut-point belongs to the better of the two tertiles.

## Task 2: t3_rating, t3_confidence and the rest (your own assessment)

There is no rule for this task. Give your own overall assessment of the company's environmental performance, judged from the evidence given.

- t3_rating: an integer from 1 to 5, where 1 is very weak and 5 is very strong environmental performance.
- t3_confidence: an integer from 1 to 5, where 1 is very low and 5 is very high confidence in your t3_rating.
- top_drivers: exactly 3 different names from the list under "Names for top_drivers", the evidence items that most influenced your t3_rating, most influential first.
- justification: at most 40 words. Every claim in it must name the indicator or KPI it relies on.
- insufficient_evidence: true if the evidence given is not enough for you to give a trustworthy t3_rating, otherwise false.
