# Usability check and buyer/lender review (protocol, fixed before anyone is asked)

Purpose: give two supporting claims some evidence. **Gap 4 and the data-entry design (gap 1):** can a small
manufacturer's manager with ordinary bills enter data and understand the trust label? **Gap 7:** would a
buyer or lender find the one-page report useful? This is a small qualitative check, reported separately from
H1-H5. It does not test emissions accuracy, and with 3-5 people nothing here is a statistic. Do not report
percentages or p-values from it.

Rules: the tasks, questions and pass criteria below are fixed now. If you change one after running a
session, log the date and reason at the bottom of this file and say so in the paper. Report every session,
including the ones that went badly.

## A. Usability check (3-5 people, 25-30 minutes each)

**Who.** Owners or managers of small manufacturing units, ideally in the chosen sector (textile processing).
If none are reachable, use people with similar duties (accounts or stores staff in a small factory) and say so.
Classmates are a last resort and must be labelled as such: they are not users.

**Setup.** The running app, on the illustrative example or on the person's own bills (their choice). Screen
and voice recorded only with written consent; otherwise the observer takes notes. Observer does not help
unless the person is stuck for 2 minutes, and records each time help was given.

**Tasks** (read aloud one at a time, no hints):

| # | Task | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enter one month of electricity and one fuel from a bill | both rows saved with a quantity, unit and rupee value |
| 2 | Say how sure you are about the electricity figure, using the app | person selects a label and explains why (measured, estimated, or default) |
| 3 | Find the total emissions for the month | correct figure read from the Summary page |
| 4 | Find the activity that causes most emissions | names it from the Hotspots page |
| 5 | Change one number from Default to Measured and say what changed | notices the trust index and uncertainty moved |
| 6 | Try one what-if and say whether you would act on it | uses the What-if page and gives a reason |
| 7 | Produce the one-page report | report page opened or downloaded |

**Record per task:** completed unaided / with help / not completed; minutes; what they said or clicked
that was not expected.

**Questions after the tasks** (ask in this order, write the answer in their words):

1. In your own words, what does "Estimated" mean here? What does "Default" mean?
2. Which of your numbers would you trust least, and does the app agree?
3. Did any word or screen confuse you? Which?
4. Would you keep entering data monthly? What would stop you?
5. What record or number did you wish to enter but could not?

**Comprehension scoring (fixed now).** Question 1 is correct if "Estimated" is described as worked out from
something else (a bill, hours, price) and "Default" as a typical value not from this unit. Two readers score
it independently if possible.

**Pass criteria for the design (placeholders, fixed now):** at least 3 of 5 people finish tasks 1-4
unaided, and at least 3 of 5 describe Estimated and Default correctly. Missing either is reported as a
finding about the interface, not hidden.

## B. Buyer or lender review (1-3 people, 20 minutes each)

**Who.** A procurement or sustainability person at a company that buys from small manufacturers, or a bank
or NBFC credit officer who lends to them. Not family, not your teacher.

**Material.** The one-page report from the illustrative example (clearly labelled as example, not real
data), plus the sentence on the page that the figures are estimates and not certified.

**Questions:**

1. What would you do with this page? Who would read it?
2. Which number or line would you rely on, and which would you ignore?
3. Does the trust label change how you read a figure?
4. Would you accept this in place of an audited or certified figure? If not, what else would you want?
5. What is missing? What would make you stop reading?

**Report as** quotes and themes, one paragraph each, with the person's role and sector (not their name).
Do not write that buyers or lenders "accept" the report; at most, that the people asked said X.

## C. Ethics and data handling

- Written consent first; the person may stop at any time and skip any question.
- Collect no names, phone numbers, firm names or GST numbers. Use P1, P2, ... and the role only.
- Real bills entered during a session stay on the participant's device or are deleted afterwards; none go
  into the repository. The app keeps data in the browser session only.
- Recordings are deleted after notes are written, unless the participant agrees to keep them for the report.
- If your institution requires approval for interviews, get it before the first session.

## D. What goes in the paper

One short subsection: who was asked (number, role, sector), the seven tasks, completions and help counts as
counts (for example "3 of 4 finished tasks 1-4 unaided"), comprehension of the two labels, the main
confusions and the changes made afterwards, and the buyer/lender themes. State that the sample is tiny,
self-selected and not representative.
