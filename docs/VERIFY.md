## Design notes that are not verification items

- **Masked company codes** (`CEM-03` etc.) are assigned when a company is first ingested, ordered by a
  seeded hash of its NSE symbol (never by market cap or input order, so the number carries no size
  information) and never reused or changed. If you ingest more than 5 candidates per sector, the
  candidates also get codes. Phase 3 sample selection will renumber the selected companies 01..N
  before any prompt is rendered, and that is the last time codes change.
- **Quantile method** for sector benchmarks and T2 tertile cut-points: linear interpolation between
  order statistics (numpy's default). With 5 companies per sector the cut-points are coarse. A value
  that equals a cut-point takes the better tertile (fixed in Phase 4, stated in the prompt).
